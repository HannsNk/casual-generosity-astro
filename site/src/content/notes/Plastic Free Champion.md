Most plastic-free advice is either abstract ("reduce your footprint") or all-or-nothing (a full zero-waste lifestyle overhaul). Neither actually changes what you do on a Tuesday. What's worked better for me is thinking about it in four smaller modes, and picking off one at a time rather than trying to do all of it at once.

**Prepared** means the boring, unglamorous setup that makes everything else easier — bin bags folded into every backpack, so a walk can turn into a cleanup without any planning. **Informed** means actually knowing where the plastic sneaks in, which a plastic-free picnic makes obvious fast — you'll be surprised what turns out to be avoidable once you're paying attention, and what turns out to be genuinely hard. **Proactive** and **reactive** are two sides of the same coin: proactive is picking up litter as part of a hike you were doing anyway; reactive is doing it because you happened to walk past a park that needed it.

None of this requires becoming the person who lectures people about straws. It's mostly just carrying the right two-pound item and noticing a bit more than you used to.
